The starting point

Three kinds
of context.

A structural pattern can be compared with biological reference maps.

  1. 01

    Structure

    MRI-derived regional patterns describe where brain anatomy differs.

  2. 02

    Receptor context

    Reference datasets describe regional distributions of receptors and transporters.

  3. 03

    Cell-type context

    Gene-expression signatures offer another reference for investigating cellular biology.

External reference maps do not measure neurotransmitter levels, receptor density or cell activity in an individual MRI participant.

The distinction that matters

Association opens a question.
It does not settle the mechanism.

Spatial overlap can point to a biological system worth investigating. It cannot establish causation or show whether a cell is compensating, adapting or losing function.

The prototype lets you construct different functional explanations under specified assumptions. Its model is an illustration, not an estimate of a person’s cellular state.

The longer-term opportunity

From a clue
to a better test.

For a treatment claim, several kinds of evidence are still missing.

Reproduce associations in independent data. Establish what the implicated system does. Then test whether an intervention preserves useful function, and under which conditions.

A receptor association cannot tell us which medicine will work. This project has not tested a drug, established a treatment benefit or validated patient selection.

Who this is for

researchers.
