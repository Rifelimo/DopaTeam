A few useful distinctions

Before you explore.

Does StarLens diagnose Alzheimer’s?

No. It is a research prototype for exploring patterns and assumptions. Diagnostic performance, treatment selection and clinical benefit have not been established.

What data does the demo start with?

Study evidence starts with reported summaries from the two supplied papers. The regional sandbox uses 82 synthetic identifiers and 30 invented maps until aligned aggregates are imported. The separate 3D atlas uses standard anatomy and illustrative overlays. No individual patient scan is displayed.

Can I bring my own research data?

The prototype accepts a supported aggregate JSON bundle under 1 MB, after validation. Confirm data rights, region order and anatomical correspondence first. Imported values stay in browser memory. The evidence export does not include the raw imported arrays.

What would count as the next scientific step?

Verify anatomical alignment and data permissions, use suitable spatial statistics, and test the proposed functional explanations with independent data and experiments.
