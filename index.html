03

The research question

A result can be clear.
Its meaning can still be uncertain.

Imagine a research team finds a pattern of structural brain changes. Comparing that pattern with maps of neurotransmitter systems gives it a possible biological lead. The team now needs to decide what that finding means and what to investigate next.

The Life Sciences challenge asks what evidence distinguishes adaptation, compensation and dysfunction in Alzheimer’s disease. DopaTeam addresses one part: when two explanations fit the observations, which additional measurement could separate them?

Are cells maintaining function, or failing to maintain it?

Our biological motivation is a population of inhibitory neurons, called somatostatin or SST interneurons, that help regulate brain activity. We ask how compensation might be distinguished from dysfunction. This is a motivating hypothesis: the current imaging comparison does not identify those cells or measure their function.

Published work on distinct interneuron activity in a mouse model provides biological context. It is not a human validation of this simulation.

The brief explicitly accepts a carefully stated counterexample and an experiment design. Our contribution is to show what the chosen observations leave unresolved and compare measurements that could resolve it under the model’s assumptions.

In the demonstration, the imaging inputs look identical in both scenarios. The assumed useful output of the circuit is different.

SCENARIO A

Function is preserved.

A stronger assumed response maintains the modelled output. This is compatible with compensation under the assumptions.

SCENARIO B

Function is reduced.

A weaker assumed response leaves output below a stated threshold. This is compatible with dysfunction in the example.

Here, function means useful output in normalized model units. The demonstration threshold is 0.8 and has no clinical interpretation. These scenarios are constructed explanations, not measured patient or cell states. Separating adaptation from compensation would require additional evidence about how function is maintained.

Before collecting more data, the useful question is whether those data could distinguish the explanations we already have.

Who would use this?

The intended user is an Alzheimer’s research team choosing an experiment after an imaging finding. The proposed benefit is a clearer reason for that choice, with the assumptions visible before time and resources are committed.

04

The work completed

What has actually been built.

The work produced a browser prototype with three connected views: Molecular context, Competing states and Next experiment. It turns the research question into a small example whose assumptions and calculations can be inspected.

  1. A way to explore imaging patterns and reference maps.

    The molecular view compares values across 82 simulated regions with 30 illustrative reference maps. It ranks how closely the patterns correspond and checks how the result changes when one region is left out. These comparisons describe an association; they do not establish its biological cause.

  2. Two explanations that share the same observations.

    The competing states view keeps the imaging inputs and reference maps fixed. The user changes assumptions about functional response and sees the resulting output change. This makes the information missing from those observations visible.

  3. A comparison of three additional measurements.

    The next experiment view compares another structural observation, another atlas score and an independent measure of function. It shows which of these three predefined options separates the constructed pair and how that separation changes as measurement noise increases.

  4. A reproducible calculation and a local record of the result.

    The same numerical example has an independent Python implementation alongside the browser calculations. The prototype can export its parameters, outputs and comparison scores to a local file so the reasoning can be reviewed.

  5. A complete demonstration that works with synthetic data.

    The portable example contains invented imaging and reference values. It needs no patient records or model API. The full prototype also supports an optional local import of compatible research aggregates. Importing those data supplies context; it does not validate the functional model.

How the parts fit together

The full prototype uses React and Vite for the interface, JavaScript for the interactive calculations and Python for an independent numerical check. The reference map comparison and the functional simulation are separate: a high map association does not set the functional response or establish a cell state.

This HTML page is a standalone explanation with a smaller illustration of the same model. Its text, styling and interaction are contained in this file, so it can be opened without installing the full prototype.

See the calculation and its assumptions

The demonstration assumes functional output = substrate × response. Both scenarios use the same hypothetical substrate, 0.72. This variable is not a measurement of MRI volume or cell count.

Scenario A: 0.72 × 1.40 = 1.008
Scenario B: 0.72 × 0.80 = 0.576

The expected output difference is 0.432. A demonstration threshold of 0.8 puts one output above the threshold and the other below it. That threshold is arbitrary and has no clinical interpretation.

At noise 0.10, the expected difference divided by noise is 4.32.

Noise is the assumed standard deviation of one measurement. Each shaded band extends that distance on either side of the expected reading. These bands are illustrative, not observed data or confidence intervals. Separation divided by noise is not a probability of diagnosis or a measure of clinical accuracy.

06

The evidence so far

What was checked, and what it tells us.

The prototype was checked at two levels: whether the calculations behave as intended and whether the software works. These checks support a functioning demonstration. They do not establish that it improves real research decisions.

Results from the constructed example and software verification.
CheckResult and meaning
Keep the observations fixedImaging inputs and reference maps remain identical while the assumed functional outputs differ. The available observations cannot distinguish this constructed pair.
Add a functional readingThe expected readings are 1.008 and 0.576. The gap is 0.432 in illustrative units. The added measurement separates this pair in the noiseless model.
Increase measurement noiseDoubling noise from 0.10 to 0.20 changes the gap divided by noise from 4.32 to 2.16. The underlying outputs stay the same; the measurement becomes less informative relative to its uncertainty.
Check the implementationAll 12 JavaScript tests and 12 independent Python tests passed in the latest check. The production build also passed. These checks establish numerical and software consistency for the tested cases, separate from biological validation.

Where the evidence stops.

The model deliberately excludes the functional response from the observations. The result therefore depends on that assumption. Another acquisition or another model could provide additional information.

The current prototype does not determine which explanation is true in a patient. It compares predefined measurement types, rather than choosing a specific laboratory assay. Separating this pair does not identify every hidden variable or establish when a cell changes state. Its usefulness for experimental planning still needs to be tested.

07

From a demo to useful evidence

What would make this useful in practice?

The next step is to test the proposed value directly. A convincing demonstration is a starting point. It needs to lead to a better research decision and then to a measurement that works in an experimental system.

1

Test whether it improves the choice of measurement.

Give researchers the same question and information. Compare those using DopaTeam with those using a static presentation. Assess whether they identify the unresolved explanations, choose a measurement that could distinguish them and explain why. This would test the value of making the assumptions explicit.

2

Test the proposed measurement in a suitable biological system.

Define useful circuit function in a system where the relevant cells are identified and their output can be measured independently. Separate donors across development and evaluation. Compare simple structural and molecular explanations, including whether disease severity, tissue composition or batch effects account for the result. A network activity measurement alone would not establish SST cell function or compensation.

The intended outcome is a better justified next experiment. Any claim of saved time, lower cost or improved scientific results would need evidence from that next stage.

09

Questions you may be asked

A few distinctions worth being clear about.

What has already been published?

Ricardo is the first author of the 2025 neuroimaging study linked above. It provides scientific context for the question. The hackathon prototype and its new simulation have not been validated by that publication.

What is a neurotransmitter map?

In this project, the term refers to a reference pattern related to a neurotransmitter system across brain regions. The molecular view compares that pattern with a regional imaging pattern. The portable demonstration uses invented values for both.

A reference map supplies context. It does not measure an individual patient’s neurotransmitter levels or establish how a particular cell population is functioning.

Why are the map comparison and the functional model separate?

They answer different questions. The map comparison describes how two regional patterns correspond. The functional model explores what can happen under two assumed responses. The present prototype does not infer the response from a map score, and it does not establish a biological mechanism linking the two.

Does DopaTeam find the right explanation?

The current example shows why the starting observations leave two explanations open. It then compares additional measurements that might separate them under the chosen assumptions. Establishing the correct explanation in a real system requires independent evidence.

Is the result surprising if the model was built that way?

The mathematical result follows from the assumptions. The proposed contribution is to make those assumptions inspectable and connect an unresolved explanation to a measurement question. Whether that helps researchers make better choices is a separate claim that can be tested.

What is complete, and what remains to be done?

The working software, synthetic example, measurement comparison, independent numerical reference and local export are complete as a prototype. The next work is to assess research usefulness and test an appropriate functional measurement in an experimental system.

Are these patient results?

No. The portable demonstration and the illustration on this page use simulated values. The optional local aggregate importer is a separate feature. Imported research context does not turn the simulation into a validated patient model.

The question is simple. The useful work is making the answer testable.
