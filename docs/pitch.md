# DopaTeam spoken pitch

About 90 seconds at a steady pace with pauses.

I’m Ricardo, and I’m building DopaTeam for Alzheimer’s research.

Imagine you have a brain imaging result that fits two different explanations. In one, function is still preserved. In the other, it is reduced. Before running another experiment, you need to know what measurement could tell those explanations apart.

That is the decision DopaTeam is designed to support.

It brings imaging data and neurotransmitter reference maps into an interactive workspace. In the current demo, two simulated scenarios have exactly the same imaging inputs but different functional outputs. The user compares additional measurements and sees which one separates those scenarios under the model’s assumptions.

The demo also shows why measurement quality matters. As noise increases, the difference becomes harder to distinguish.

The intended benefit is a clearer reason to choose the next experiment, with the assumptions visible before time and resources are committed.

I have built a working prototype with simulated data. The next step is to test whether it improves researchers’ measurement choices, then assess those choices in a suitable experimental system.

DopaTeam starts with a practical question: what evidence would actually change our conclusion?

## Demonstration cue

Start with the two scenarios. Switch to the independent functional measurement and increase noise once. Close with the plan to test research usefulness and then the measurement itself.
